## Inline and display math

The Hamiltonian of a simple pendulum is $H = \tfrac{1}{2}p^2 - \cos q$, which gives Hamilton's
equations

$$
\dot q = \frac{\partial H}{\partial p} = p, \qquad
\dot p = -\frac{\partial H}{\partial q} = -\sin q .
$$
